# Product fit and adoption

## Which segment has the clearest job and product fit for Hunch today?

### Takeaway
Product sellers have the clearer measurable job for the current Hunch design: find which buyer situation, message, and post can produce orders, leads, or messages. Creators are a valid market only if Hunch narrows the first creator segment to people with an identifiable offer or monetization path; “grow my audience” by itself produces weaker business proof and needs different success metrics.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok explicitly distinguishes creators seeking visibility/followers, businesses seeking awareness/leads, and TikTok Shop sellers seeking sales. Its Promote tool exposes separate account-growth, lead, sales, and LIVE goals. [TikTok Promote](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-promote-on-tiktok?lang=en-GB)
- TikTok lets advertisers select automatic or manual targeting, with manual dimensions including location, age, gender, language, interests/behaviors, audience, spending power, and device; it warns that precise controls may limit delivery and hurt performance. [TikTok targeting](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-targeting-for-your-upgraded-smart-experience)
- TikTok’s official Ads Manager targeting documentation says targeting options are configured at the ad-group level and available locations vary by market. [TikTok Ads Manager targeting](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/ad-targeting)
- TikTok Promote has ready-made promotion packs with preset budget, duration, and audience, as well as custom promotions. [TikTok Promote](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-promote-on-tiktok?lang=en-GB)

### Inferences
- For a seller, Hunch’s existing chain (product → buyer moments → localized product posts → paid test → cost/result or clicks) maps to a transaction outcome. For a creator focused on followers, the same data model and “buyer audience” cards do not fit: it needs content-view, watch-time, profile-visit, follower-conversion, saves/shares, or offer-conversion outcomes, depending on what the creator is trying to earn.
- The best creator wedge is not all influencers. Start with creators who have something to sell or convert (affiliate offers, services/coaching, digital products, live commerce, or creator-led shops); test audience/content angles against qualified clicks, leads, or purchases. This preserves a measurable ROI story while serving creators.
- Native promotion products already package the basic creator workflow (boost a post, pick a goal/audience/budget). Hunch must add a repeatable hypothesis and learning loop, not merely restate native audience settings.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable Philippines-specific public comparison of creators’ paid promotion ROI against small sellers’ paid promotion ROI.
- Official TikTok targeting support does not establish that all listed targeting controls or Promote features are available in the Philippines or to every account; confirm on actual Philippine accounts before designing a test workflow.

## How crowded is the creator-tool alternative set, and what must Hunch do differently?

### Takeaway
Creator-facing publishing, analytics, AI ideation, and caption writing are already bundled by established tools and by the platforms themselves. Hunch’s defensible distinction should be helping a creator decide which audience promise/creative earns meaningful outcomes, then converting that result into the next content/offer test.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram has a Professional Dashboard Best Practices hub for creators with personalized account tips across creation, engagement, reach, and monetization. [Meta announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/)
- TikTok Studio is promoted by TikTok as a free creator tool that centralizes creation tools, workflow capabilities, and analytics. [TikTok newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/helping-creators-bring-creativity-to-life-with-tiktok-studio?lang=en)
- Later’s current annual-billing Starter plan is listed at $18.75/month and includes one user, scheduling, AI content tools, platform analytics, and up to three months of analytics history; Growth lists $37.50/month and adds collaboration, more publishing volume, AI credits, and longer analytics history. [Later pricing](https://later.com/pricing/)
- Buffer lists Essentials at $5/month for one channel billed annually, including unlimited scheduled posts and one user; its paid Team offering lists advanced analytics, AI assistance, and collaboration features. [Buffer pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- Competing on generic AI captions, a calendar, or basic analytics would place Hunch against free platform-native tools and low-cost schedulers.
- A creator-specific Hunch should frame its output around content hypotheses (promise, format, hook, audience need, and offer), compare organic and paid results where possible, and support measurable creator outcomes rather than assume every audience is a purchaser of the creator’s own product.
- For monetized creators, one useful plan could compare a small number of audience/creative hypotheses across a chosen outcome (qualified profile visits, link clicks, DMs/leads, affiliate conversions, or sales). For pure audience-growth creators, use profile visits/follows and make the weaker downstream monetization evidence explicit.

### Gaps
- Public product pages establish feature overlap but do not reveal retention, willingness to pay, or efficacy for Philippine micro-creators.
- No public data located that quantifies Hunch’s differentiability against creator-growth platforms in the Philippines.

## Which segment is likely to adopt more easily, and what would change in the MVP?

### Takeaway
Based on the current product and workflow, sellers should be the easier first adoption test: Hunch can tie an ad spend to a concrete business result, and its current Elle journey already reflects that. Creators may be easier to recruit through creator friends and may have an immediately recognizable content pain, but that is a channel hypothesis—not evidence of paid adoption—and their varied goals increase product and measurement friction.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok’s own positioning separates creator visibility/following goals from seller sales and business lead goals, reinforcing that these are different jobs with different desired outcomes. [TikTok Promote](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-promote-on-tiktok?lang=en-GB)
- TikTok notes that switching from automatic to more precise custom targeting may limit delivery and impact campaign performance. [TikTok targeting](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-targeting-for-your-upgraded-smart-experience)
- Later and Buffer already sell the adjacent creator/small-business workflows—publishing, AI content assistance, analytics, and calendars—at low monthly entry points, while TikTok Studio is free. [Later](https://later.com/pricing/), [Buffer](https://buffer.com/pricing), [TikTok Studio](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/helping-creators-bring-their-creativity-to-life-with-tiktok-studio?lang=en)

### Inferences
- Seller adoption barriers: budget sensitivity, unfamiliarity with Ads Manager, and the fact that a valid test needs a consistent objective, audience labels, spend, and conversion tracking. Hunch’s guided manual test helps, but an ₱800 ad budget may be a real obstacle for micro-sellers.
- Creator adoption barriers: some already have free native analytics; many may not spend on paid promotion; and “better reach” or “more followers” does not prove income. For creators selling something, adoption can be easier if Hunch integrates the test with an affiliate link, landing page, lead form, booking page, or sales metric they already use.
- The smallest creator pivot is to keep the seven-step flow but change “product” to “offer/content niche,” audience cards to viewer/customer hypotheses, posts to platform-native concepts (especially short-form video scripts and hooks), and the results model to support views/retention/profile visits/follows/clicks/leads/conversions. Do not rebuild the complete product until pilot interviews show which of those outcomes creators will pay to improve.
- A practical go-to-market experiment is a paired pilot: recruit 5–10 sellers and 5–10 monetized creators, observe completion of the same audience/content test, then compare completion, willingness to spend on promotion, and willingness to pay for the next test. Treat these as proposed experiment numbers, not market facts.

### Gaps
- No direct Hunch pilot evidence yet establishes which segment completes a test, pays for promotion, or renews.
- “Creators are easier to recruit” is only an inference from the team’s stated influencer-friend network; the team has not supplied counts, interview results, or purchase commitments.
- Exact platform metric exports and attribution for creator goals should be validated with real Instagram, TikTok, and Meta accounts before selecting a final cross-platform result schema.
